Privacy Policy

Who we are

Our website address is: http://www.grabotech.com

1. Comments

In the event that a comment is left on the site, the following data shall be collected: the data submitted within the comments form, the commenter's IP address, and the browser user agent string. This collection is undertaken solely for the purposes of spam detection.

2. User-Uploaded Media

Users are advised that the uploading of images containing embedded location data (EXIF GPS) should be avoided. It is noted that any location data present in images on the website may be subject to download and extraction by visitors thereof.

3. Cookie Policy

3.1 Voluntary Storage of Comment Details:

Users may opt-in to the storage of their name, email address, and website in cookies when submitting a comment. This is provided as a convenience to avoid re-entering this information for future comments. Such cookies herein shall have a duration of one (1) year.

3.2 Authentication & Session Management:

A temporary cookie shall be set upon visiting the login page for the sole purpose of determining browser cookie acceptance. This cookie contains no personal data and is automatically discarded upon the closure of the browser.

Following a successful login, multiple cookies are established to retain login credentials and user-specific screen display options. Authentication cookies are maintained for a period of two (2) days, while display preference cookies persist for one (1) year. Selection of the "Remember Me" option extends the login persistence to two (2) weeks. The authentication cookies are revoked upon logout.

3.3 Editorial Session Cookie:

If a user edits or publishes an article, an additional cookie is stored in the browser. This cookie contains no personal data and solely signifies the post ID of the article being edited. Its validity period is one (1) day.

4. Embedded Third-Party Content

This site may display embedded content (including, but not limited to, videos, images, and articles) originating from external websites. Such embedded content shall be construed as directing the User to the respective third-party website.

The providers of these websites may collect information about you, utilize cookies, incorporate additional third-party tracking technologies, and monitor your interaction with the embedded content. Interaction monitoring may occur, without limitation, in cases where you maintain an account and are authenticated on the said third-party website.

5. Data Protection Clause

Article 1: Data Disclosure

User data shall be treated as confidential. Disclosure of personal information to third parties is limited to the specific purposes herein defined. In the event of a password reset request, the User's IP address shall be included in the corresponding reset email communication.

Article 2: Data Retention Period

Personal data shall be retained only for the period necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected.

Section 2.1: Comments and associated metadata are retained indefinitely. This facilitates the automatic approval of subsequent comments, thereby bypassing moderation queues.

Section 2.2: For registered users, the personal information provided in their user profile shall be stored for the duration of the account's existence. Users may access, amend, or request the deletion of their personal data at any time, with the exception of the username. Website administrators retain the ability to access and edit this information.

Article 3: Data Subject Rights

Users who maintain an account or have previously submitted comments on this site possess the following rights:

To request and receive a structured, machine-readable copy of the personal data held by the Controller.

To request the erasure of all personal data concerning the User.

These rights are subject to limitations and do not extend to data which the Controller is mandated to retain for compliance with legal, administrative, or security obligations.

Article 4: International Data Transfer

User data may be processed in jurisdictions outside the User's country of residence. Specifically, visitor comments are subject to review by an automated spam detection service, the infrastructure of which may be located internationally.

Article 5: Contact Information

For any inquiries regarding this policy, to exercise your data rights, or to lodge a complaint, the Data Controller can be contacted via electronic mail at: sales@grabotech.com.
